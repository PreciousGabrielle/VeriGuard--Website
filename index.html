Why VeriGuard

Scams are getting harder to spot

Fake links, messages and QR codes are designed to look real. One wrong click can cost you your passwords, your money or your personal information.

The Problem

It's hard to tell real from fake

  • Phishing links copy the look of real brands and websites
  • QR codes hide where they really lead
  • Urgent messages pressure you to act before you think
  • Most people have no quick, simple way to check
Our Solution

A quick second opinion, in your pocket

  • Scan a link, QR code or screenshot in seconds
  • Get a clear risk level written in plain language
  • See why it's risky and compare it with the real thing
  • Follow step-by-step actions to stay safe

Built for everyday internet users

Unlike security tools made for IT experts, VeriGuard explains every result in plain language and tells you exactly what to do next.

  • Students
  • Families & older adults
  • Online shoppers
  • Anyone with suspicious messages
Features

Everything you need to check before you click

One app to inspect suspicious content, understand the risk, and learn how to stay safe online.

Link Scanning

Paste any URL and VeriGuard checks the domain, connection and wording for common phishing patterns.

QR Code Scanning

See where a QR code really leads before you open it, so a sticker on a poster can't trick you.

File & Screenshot Scanning

Upload a screenshot, image, PDF or DOCX that contains a suspicious message and get it analyzed.

Clear Risk Levels

Every result is labelled High Risk, Caution or Safe, with a short summary of what was found.

Recommended Actions

Step-by-step guidance on what to do next, from reporting the message to changing your password.

Knowledge Center

Short, practical guides on phishing, QR code safety and safe browsing habits.

About Us

The team behind VeriGuard

We are Vericore Solutions. VeriGuard began as our capstone project, built around one simple question: how can everyday people tell whether a link, message or QR code is safe before they open it?

Our Mission

To help everyday internet users recognize and avoid phishing and online scams through simple, accessible verification tools and clear guidance.

Our Vision

A digital world where everyone can browse, message and scan with confidence, and where no one falls for a scam because they couldn't tell it was fake.

Our Objectives

  1. Help users identify suspicious messages, links, websites and QR codes.
  2. Present results in clear, easy-to-understand language.
  3. Raise awareness of phishing attacks and online scams.
  4. Encourage smarter, safer decisions online.
